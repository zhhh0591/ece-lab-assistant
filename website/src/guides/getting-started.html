Where things are

  • The lab is the ECE Makerspace & Capstone Laboratory.
  • The lab technicians are in their office next to the lab. Knock on the door for help with computers, equipment, software, tools and supplies.
  • Every bench has an oscilloscope, a DC power supply, a function generator, a Fluke 45 multimeter, a PC and a toolbox labeled with the bench number.
  • Larger tools are in the red shared tool cabinet. Ask a technician.
  • First aid kits are at the station by the sink. See Safety.

The rules

  1. No food or drink in the lab.
  2. Follow the Makerspaces honor code.
  3. Ask before using any machine for the first time: 3D printers, PCB tools, reflow oven, chargers.
  4. Put every tool back in your bench toolbox before you leave, and check it is all there when you arrive.
  5. If something is broken, put a note on it and report it. Do not leave it for the next team to find.
  6. Turn off soldering irons, supply outputs and instruments when you leave.

Before you power anything up

  • Set a current limit on the power supply before turning on the output.
  • Check the scope probe is set to the same 1x or 10x as the channel.
  • Set the function generator load to High Z, or its amplitude reads half of what you get.

Getting help

Knock at the technicians' office first. If nobody answers, message a technician on Teams (preferred) or email them. Names are on the Contact page.
