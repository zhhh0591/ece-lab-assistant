PCB milling and assembly

The LPKF ProtoMat S104 turns your PCB layout into a finished board, ready for parts. Only lab technicians operate the machine: you prepare the files, they mill the board.

What the mill can do

  • Standard FR4 board with 1 oz copper. FR4 is free for students. For any other material, buy it yourself.
  • Single-sided or double-sided boards.
  • No plated through-holes. To connect top and bottom, thread a wire through a hole or around the board edge.
  • Precision is ±0.1 mm. Keep at least 0.5 mm clearance around your traces.
  • The largest board that fits in the reflow oven is 8 x 6.5 in (203 x 165 mm).

The mill makes traces by cutting away the copper around them, leaving only your circuit paths. Review your layout carefully before you submit it; every mistake costs material and machine time.

Files to send

Gerber files (.gbr) are preferred. Other formats, like DXF, often get corrupted on import. A double-sided board needs at least these four files:

FileWhat it isPut this in the nameExample
TopTop copper layerTOP (capitals)logic_card_TOP.gbr
BottomBottom copper layerBOTlogic_card_BOT.gbr
DrillEvery through-hole. Must be an Excellon file.DRLlogic_card_DRL.drl
OutlineThe board edge, where it is cut out of the panelBOAlogic_card_BOA.gbr

A single-sided board needs one copper layer (TOP or BOT), the drill file and the outline.

Check your files before you send them

Drop your Gerber and drill files here. The checker reads them in your browser; nothing is uploaded. It checks the names, the file types, and the board size against the rules above. It cannot check trace clearance, so still review your layout in your CAD tool.

Assembly: placement and reflow

For surface-mount boards the lab also has a placement machine and an LPKF ProtoFlow E reflow oven. Ask a technician before using either for the first time.

When to use an outside PCB house

The mill can be temperamental. If your schedule has no room for error, consider a quick-turn PCB manufacturer. You pay for it, but you get design feedback, a firm schedule and fast turnaround, and they can make plated through-holes.
