Make & Print

3D printing

Two Prusa MK4 printers, PRUSA_3D_1 and PRUSA_3D_2. PLA only. You run your own print, so read these rules first.

The rules

  1. If you think a printer is not working properly, put a note on the machine and report it on Teams to a technician, or knock on the door of the technicians' office. You can also use the report form.
  2. If you are unsure how to use the machine, ask first. Contact a technician or knock at the technicians' office.
  3. Slice with PrusaSlicer, using the settings below.
  4. Use only PLA filament.
  5. Check on your print as often as you can to make sure it is running properly. The live video below helps.
  6. Take care of the machines. Do not force, bend or adjust any part of the printer by hand. Future students will need them for their capstone projects too.

Watch your print live

Each printer streams video, and several people can watch at once. You need to be on the campus network or the VPN.

  1. Install VLC media player on your PC or Mac.
  2. In VLC, open Media → Open Network Stream (on a Mac: File → Open Network).
  3. Paste the address for your printer and press Play.

Tips for a print that works

  • Export your part as STL or 3MF from your CAD tool.
  • Watch the first layer. Most failed prints fail there.
  • Parts with large overhangs need supports. Turn them on in PrusaSlicer, or rotate the part so it needs fewer.
  • Do not touch the print sheet with your fingers. Oil makes prints come loose.
  • Let the sheet cool, then flex it gently to release the print.
