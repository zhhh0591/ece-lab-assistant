Report a problem

A blown meter fuse, a missing tool, a printer acting up, a wrong page on this site. Telling us is how it gets fixed before the next team finds it.

What kind of problem?
What you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead.

This opens your own email app with the report filled in, addressed to the lab technicians. Nothing is sent until you press Send there, and this site stores nothing.

Faster for urgent problems

Knock on the door at the technicians' office, or message a technician on Teams.
