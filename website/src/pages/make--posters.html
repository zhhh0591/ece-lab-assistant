Poster printing

Capstone trifold presentation posters are printed for free. Send the final PDF by email, then pick it up in the technicians' office.

How it works

  1. Make sure the PDF is the final version.
  2. Email it to {{site:poster_email}} and include the poster's dimensions.
  3. Your print should be ready in the technicians' office within 2 days of the email.

Write the email

Fill this in and press the button. It opens your email app with everything filled in. Attach your PDF there, then send.

Your PDF's page size should match the dimensions you type here. In most PDF viewers you can check it under File → Properties.
