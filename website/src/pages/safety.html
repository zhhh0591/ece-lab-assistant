Safety

Know these before you start working. If anything feels unsafe, stop and get a technician from the technicians' office.

First aid

First aid kits are at the first aid station by the sink in the lab. Look for the red FIRST AID KIT signs with the blue arrow.

If you use something from a kit, tell a technician so it can be restocked.

Lab rules

  • No food or drink in the lab.
  • Follow the Makerspaces honor code.
  • Ask before using any machine for the first time.
  • If something is broken, put a note on it and report it. Do not try to repair lab equipment yourself.
  • Turn off irons, supplies and instruments when you leave your bench.

Soldering

  • Wear eye protection. Solder can spit.
  • Use the ducted hood to vent fumes.
  • Use rosin-free and lead-free solder whenever possible.
  • Keep cleaning solvents in their dispensing bottles.
  • Wash your hands with soap and water after soldering.
  • Keep a safe workspace: handle the iron correctly and keep cords and power safe.
  • Inspect the soldering equipment for damage before each use.
  • Never touch the hot parts of a soldering iron.
  • Switch the iron off when you are not using it.

Battery charging stations

  • Read the charger's instruction manual before you charge anything.
  • Do not modify, customize or alter any cables or connectors.
  • Do not leave batteries charging or connected over the weekend.
  • If you need a longer or balance-charging cable, ask the technicians.
  • Questions? Go to the technicians' office.

3D printers

  • The nozzle and bed are hot. Keep your hands out while the printer is moving.
  • Use only PLA, and do not force or adjust any part of the printer by hand.
  • Full rules: 3D printing.

Bench instruments

  • Clip the oscilloscope ground lead to circuit ground only. It is connected to earth.
  • Set a current limit on the power supply before you turn the output on.
  • Never put a multimeter set to current across a voltage source.
  • Do not measure mains voltage on the bench.
