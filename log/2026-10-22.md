# 2026-10-22

I started a full bench test on a new MaUWB_DW3000 board.

First I checked the instruments. The Fluke 45 leads read 0.2 Ω shorted and
stayed steady when I wiggled them. I set the E3647A to 5.000 V with a 0.200 A
limit and shorted the output, and it held 0.2 A in constant current. With the
output open, the Fluke read 4.997 V.

Then the board, powered off. I found no visible damage. The 3.3 V rail read
150 kΩ to ground at 5 seconds and was still climbing, so it is not shorted. My
first reading drifted down, so I checked my setup and measured again. The
board has no 5 V test point, so the current limit will have to protect that
rail at power-up.

Next: power the board up with the current limit.
