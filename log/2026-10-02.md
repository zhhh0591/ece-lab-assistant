# 2026-10-02

I studied the bench skills I need for testing the other lab boards, from six
videos by SparkFun, EEVblog, UNSW, element14 and Adamant IT. What I learned:

- Current is measured in series, with the red lead in the A jack. That jack is
  nearly a short, 0.2 Ω on EEVblog's meter, so across a supply it blows the
  fuse.
- Resistance is measured with the power off. In EEVblog's video a 10 kΩ
  resistor read 3.6 kΩ in circuit and 10 kΩ with one leg lifted.
- A bench supply holds the set voltage until the current reaches the limit,
  then holds the current. In UNSW's example, a 3.3 Ω load with a 1 A limit
  gets about 3.3 V when set to 5 V.
- To find a fault on a dead board, follow the power from the input and check
  each rail's resistance to ground with the power off.
