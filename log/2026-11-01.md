# 2026-11-01

I tested the second MaUWB_DW3000 board and found a problem. With the power off,
I measured its 5 V rail to ground through a Seeed XIAO nRF52840 breakout and a
USB-C cable. The reading started near 25 kΩ, dropped to about 100 Ω and kept
falling. The XIAO alone and the board's 3.3 V rail both read normal, so the low
path is on the board's 5 V side or in the cable. I have not powered the board.

Next: confirm it. I will measure the first board the same way, swap the cable,
and reverse the meter leads on the second board.
