# 2026-10-28

I picked a Seeed XIAO nRF52840 to use as a USB-C breakout, so I can power the
MaUWB_DW3000 from the E3647A with a current limit.

I read Seeed's wiki for it. The page covers four versions, so I matched the
pin map to the board I have. In the pin map, the Function column gives each
pin's net name in the schematic. It lists the 5V pin as VBUS, "Power
Input/Output", and the 3V3 pin as output only, so 5V is the pin to feed. The
schematic links are under Resources. The FAQ and the pin map disagree on the
charge LED's color, so I go by the schematic. The wiki lists no power LED, so a
dark XIAO can still be powered.

I planned the order: check the XIAO first with a short check and its own
current draw, then connect the MaUWB, measure its 5 V rail to ground, and power
up at 0.2 A. I will subtract the XIAO's own current from the total.
