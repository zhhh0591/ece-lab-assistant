# 2026-10-29

I learned to read a schematic, using Seeed's XIAO nRF52840 schematic:
component symbols, reference designators like R1, D1 and U6, and net labels,
where two wires with the same name are connected even with no line drawn
between them.

In its power section, USB VBUS goes through D1, an SDM20U40 Schottky diode, to
an SGM2040 3.3 V regulator. A battery feeds the same regulator through a
MOSFET, and D1 stops it from pushing current back into the USB port. The
revision table shows no change to the power section from v1.0 to v1.2.

D1 is rated 40 V and 250 mA, and the regulator gives 3.3 V at up to 250 mA.
Without USB, a 100 kΩ resistor pulls the MOSFET's gate to ground and the
battery switches in. The regulator turns the extra input voltage into heat, so
it is the part to touch-check at power-up.
