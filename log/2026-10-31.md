# 2026-10-31

I worked out why power fed into the XIAO's 5V pin reaches the MaUWB_DW3000. A
USB-C cable is just wires joining matching contacts, VBUS to VBUS and GND to
GND, so pin 14, both connectors and the cable form one VBUS net. Wires conduct
both ways. Only a part like a diode blocks a direction.

I also traced the XIAO's own power path for this setup. With 5 V on the pin,
the MOSFET's gate sits at 5 V and it stays off, and D1 only passes current
toward the regulator, so a battery cannot reach the MaUWB. The MaUWB's current
leaves VBUS before D1, so D1's 250 mA rating does not limit it.

"Power Input" on the pin map only says the pin can power the XIAO. The
schematic is what shows it reaches the connector. I will also confirm it with a
meter: with the XIAO on USB, pin 14 should read about 5.0 V. The supply must
never be on pin 14 while the XIAO is plugged into a computer.
