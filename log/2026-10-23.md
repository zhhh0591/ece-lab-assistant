# 2026-10-23

I worked out how to power the MaUWB_DW3000 from the E3647A with a current
limit. Its 5 V rail is only reachable through the USB-C port, so the supply
needs a USB breakout in between. I plan to try a Seeed XIAO as the breakout,
since Seeed documents its 5V pin as the 5 V from its USB-C port, and subtract
the XIAO's own current.

I soldered contact points onto GND and 3.3 V on all ten boards, so the meter
probes stay put.

I also reorganized two parts cabinets and sorted seven half-bridge load cells,
the strain gauge sensors used in bathroom scales.

Next: power the board up through the XIAO with the 0.2 A limit, then check the
rail voltages and run the self-test.
