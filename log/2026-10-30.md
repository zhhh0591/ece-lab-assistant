# 2026-10-30

I worked through the USB-C connector in the XIAO nRF52840 schematic. The
socket has two rows of contacts, A1 to A12 and B1 to B12. VBUS is on A4, A9, B4
and B9 so the plug works either way up, and the schematic draws each pair as
one pin. CC1 and CC2 each have a 5.1 kΩ resistor to ground. The metal shell
goes to ground too, which matches the MaUWB, where I used the USB shell as the
meter's ground.

The header's pin 14 carries the same VBUS net label as the connector, with no
part in between. I first read pin 12, 3V3, as the same wire, but it is a
separate net after the regulator. It is the regulator's output, so pushing
power into it would back-feed the regulator. The wiki also lists it as output
only.
